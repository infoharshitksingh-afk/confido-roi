Load your CSVs

Two files: closed deals from the CRM, and GTM spend from the GL or card feed. Messy labels are fine; that's what step 2 is for. Files stay in your browser.

Used for ROI and payback. 75% is a common SaaS assumption; change it to yours.

Columns the agent looks for

Sales CSV

Required: deal_id close_date industry products arr_usd stage lead_source. Optional: created_date (for sales cycle), deal_type (New / Expansion), customer. Common aliases like amount, ACV, source, segment are recognised.

GTM spend CSV

Required: date amount_usd and at least one of vendor / description / gl_account. Optional: target_industry, so event spend aimed at one aisle counts against that aisle.

    Industry scorecard

    Spend tagged to an industry counts against it. Untagged channel spend is split by that channel's new opportunities per industry; other untagged spend by all new opportunities.

    Channel × industry scorecard

    Which channel pays back in which industry. New ARR comes from deals each channel sourced in that industry. Spend tagged to an industry stays there; the rest of a channel's spend is split by that channel's new opportunities per industry. Deals still missing an industry are left out until reviewed.
